Trojan Horse Racing

category winner best overall

A racing game where you smash through windows, made in a 3-day in-person game jam with 30 teams.

Role Lead developer & technical designer
Team 5 people
Duration 3 days, in person
Result Won category & best overall

The short versionHow we won

problem

3 days, 30 teams

Xbox Gamecamp gave every team three days to build a game from one of three categories. We picked minesweeper.

approach

Finish early, polish late

I gave the team one goal: finish the game in day one and a half. That left the rest of the jam for polish instead of features.

result

Two wins

We won our category and took best overall out of all 30 teams.

Deep diveWhat made it feel good

Feel over features

Core first. Exploding windows and movement got most of my time.

Scoped down. A small game that felt flawless beat a big one that felt rough.

Polish day. Every player-facing system was tuned in the last day and a half.

Looking backTakeaways

Scope is a weapon

Cutting the feature list early is what made the polish phase possible.

Set a deadline inside the deadline

The day-and-a-half "game is done" milestone kept the whole team aligned.

Feel beats feature count

Judges remembered how the game felt to play, not how much was in it.
